Every barrier between your intended use and an approvable path, ranked by severity, with the approvals, studies, weeks, and cost to clear each one.

The full commenting list: planning, building, conservation authority, region, utilities. Who reviews your file and what each one requires.

09 / Questions

Asked before every first report.

Q-01What does a report include?
Eight sections for any address: the zoning verdict (as-of-right, minor variance, or ZBA), a suitability analysis with every barrier ranked by severity, the full agency circulation list, required studies with terms of reference and market costs, bylaw standards cited to the exact section, a building envelope drawn to your parcel, an all-in construction cost range, and how comparable nearby applications were decided. In 60 seconds.
Q-02Which municipalities do you cover?
43 Ontario municipalities including Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Hamilton, Oakville, Burlington, and Richmond Hill. New municipalities added monthly.
Q-03Where does the data come from?
Municipal zoning bylaws, municipal GIS parcel and zoning data, agency terms of reference, and planning decision records for every covered municipality, kept current as bylaws change. Not scraped blog content. Not a generic AI summary.
Q-04Does this replace my planning consultant?
No. It front-loads the research a consultant spends the first weeks of an engagement assembling, so you walk into pre-consultation informed and spend consultant hours on strategy instead of lookup. Planning consultants and permit expeditors are among our heaviest users.
Q-05How accurate is the zoning verdict?
The verdict is read from your municipality's own zoning fabric for the exact parcel, including holding provisions and overlays, and every bylaw standard is cited to its section so you can verify it yourself. PreBuildIQ is decision support for pre-development diligence, not legal advice.
