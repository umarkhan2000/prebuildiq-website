Markham Planning Application Fees: What Development Applications Cost

Published August 2026· Updated August 2026· 6 min read· PreBuildIQ Research Team
Quick Answer

Markham charges a fee for each planning application, and for major applications such as a Zoning By-law Amendment or Official Plan Amendment the fee combines a base amount with variable charges tied to unit count or floor area. Markham's planning application fees are set in the City's fee schedule, with major applications combining a base fee and variable charges; submissions are made through the City's ePLAN portal. Minor variance fees are far lower. Fees are set in the City fee schedule and adjusted regularly, so confirm the current amount on the official schedule before you budget.

Planning application fees are the most avoidable budgeting error in Markham development, because the headline number circulating online is rarely the number you pay. Major applications carry variable, per-unit or per-square-metre charges on top of a base fee, the schedule is adjusted regularly, and two-tier municipalities layer regional fees on top. This page explains how Markham's fees are built so you can budget from structure, then confirm the exact figure from the official schedule.

How Markham Structures Its Application Fees

Markham sets its planning application fees under Comprehensive Zoning By-law 2024-19 and the City fee schedule. The key distinction is between minor and major applications. A minor variance to the Committee of Adjustment carries a modest fixed fee. A Zoning By-law Amendment, Official Plan Amendment, or major site plan application carries a base fee plus a variable component, which is why two projects of very different size pay very different amounts for the same application type.

What Drives the Cost Up

Beyond the base fee, several factors add to what you actually pay:

  • Variable charges: major applications add a per-unit or per-square-metre amount to the base.
  • Combined applications: a concurrent Official Plan Amendment and Zoning By-law Amendment costs more than either alone.
  • Peer review: the municipality can require applicant-funded peer review of technical studies.
  • Resubmission: some fee schedules charge for additional review cycles after the first.
  • Annual adjustment: fees are typically raised each year by a cost-of-living factor.
The variable-fee trap

The number you find online is almost always the base fee only. For a major application, the variable per-unit or per-GFA charge can be several multiples of the base. Always calculate the fee from the current worksheet for your actual project size, not the headline base.

Regional and Other Fees

Markham is a lower-tier municipality within York Region. The City collects the planning application fee, and York Region co-approves Official Plan Amendments, which can carry a separate Regional fee. Because Markham uses the ePLAN portal, confirm the fee at the PRCN stage so it is paid correctly at digital submission.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a zoning by-law amendment cost in Markham?

A Zoning By-law Amendment in Markham is charged as a base fee plus a variable amount tied to unit count or floor area, so the total depends on project size. Markham's planning application fees are set in the City's fee schedule, with major applications combining a base fee and variable charges; submissions are made through the City's ePLAN portal. Because the figure changes annually and scales with the project, confirm the exact amount on the current Markham fee schedule.

What are the planning application fees in Markham?

Markham charges fees by application type: a modest fixed fee for a minor variance, and base-plus-variable fees for site plan control, Zoning By-law Amendments, and Official Plan Amendments. Fees are set in the City fee schedule under Comprehensive Zoning By-law 2024-19 and adjusted regularly. Confirm current amounts on the official schedule.

Does Markham charge extra fees on top of the base application fee?

Yes. Major applications add a variable per-unit or per-square-metre charge to the base fee, combined Official Plan and Zoning amendments cost more than either alone, and applicant-funded peer review or resubmission charges can apply. As a two-tier municipality, York Region may also charge a separate fee.

How often do Markham planning fees change?

Markham typically adjusts its planning application fees once a year, usually by a cost-of-living factor. This is why a figure from an older guide is often out of date. Always confirm the fee on the current year's official schedule before budgeting.

What is the cheapest planning application in Markham?

A minor variance, decided by the Committee of Adjustment, carries the lowest fee in Markham, typically a modest fixed amount. Major applications such as a Zoning By-law Amendment or Official Plan Amendment cost far more because they combine a high base fee with variable charges.
